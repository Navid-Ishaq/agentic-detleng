Master implementation method

Dissect. Build. Break.
Compare. Transform. Deploy.

The source material identifies the underlying concept. The DeTLeng implementation, examples, architecture, experiments and business transformation are original.

01

DISSECT

Find the real mechanism, dependencies, design choices and failure points.

02

BUILD

Create an inspectable implementation instead of preserving lecture structure.

03

BREAK

Make uncertainty, edge cases and unsafe assumptions visible.

04

COMPARE

Show meaningful alternatives and trade-offs where they change system behaviour.

05

TRANSFORM

Turn technical mechanics into realistic business and product architectures.

06

DEPLOY

State runtime, validation, privacy, monitoring and production considerations honestly.
